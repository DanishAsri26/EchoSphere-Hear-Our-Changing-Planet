How to listen

  1. Low, middle, high. Higher notes mean more: more fire, more rain, more carbon dioxide, higher seas.
  2. Silence in the rain line means almost no rain: drought.
  3. Each topic has its own instrument and its own place in your headphones. Use Mute and Solo to hear one at a time.
  4. A drum hit on the fire line marks very intense burning.
